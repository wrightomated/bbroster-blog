BB Roster is a progressive web app (PWA), which means you can install it straight from your browser and run it like a native app. You get an icon on your home screen or desktop, a full-screen window without the browser bar, and it keeps working when you're offline or on a shaky connection at the table.

Here's how to install it on common platforms.

## iPhone and iPad (Safari)

1. Open [bbroster.com](https://bbroster.com) in Safari.
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with the arrow pointing up).
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Give it a name if you like, then tap **Add**.

The BB Roster icon will appear on your home screen.

## Android (Chrome)

1. Open [bbroster.com](https://bbroster.com) in Chrome.
2. Tap the **three-dot menu** in the top right.
3. Tap **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**).
4. Confirm, and it'll drop onto your home screen and app drawer.

## Desktop (Chrome or Edge)

1. Open [bbroster.com](https://bbroster.com).
2. Look for the **install icon** on the right-hand side of the address bar (a small monitor with a down arrow).
3. Click it and choose **Install**.

If the icon isn't showing, you can also find the option under the browser's main menu.

Once it's installed, BB Roster opens in its own window and behaves just like any other app on your machine. To remove it later, uninstall it the same way you would anything else.
